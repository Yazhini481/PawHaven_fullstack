About PawHaven 🐾

Creating happier endings for pets and people.

Our Mission

Every animal deserves a second chance.

PawHaven is a pet adoption and rescue platform created to connect animals in need with people who can provide them with safe and loving homes.

We make it easier to discover adoptable pets, report animals that need help and connect shelters, volunteers and adopters.

  • ✓ Promote responsible adoption
  • ✓ Support animal rescue
  • ✓ Connect shelters and adopters
  • ✓ Encourage compassionate communities
